Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Edgeform Marketing ("Edgeform", "we") runs an affiliate programme. Creators who join it submit short-form videos for our campaigns, and we pay them based on the views those videos get. This policy explains what we collect to make that work, why, and how you get it deleted.

It covers the affiliate portal at affiliate.edgeformmarketing.com and the API behind it. It does not cover Edgeform's public marketing site or our clients' own products.

What we collect

Account details you give us. Your name, email address, phone number, country, your social handles (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube), and the payout method you want to be paid through. Your email is how you sign in — we email you a one-time link rather than storing a password.

Videos you submit. The URL of each video you enter, the platform it is on, and its ID on that platform.

View counts and earnings. For each approved video we record a weekly view count, what you earned that week, and the running totals. We keep every weekly figure as an append-only history so that a payment can always be traced back to the numbers behind it.

Screenshots you upload. Where a video's view count is not publicly visible — Instagram trial reels, for instance — you can upload a screenshot of your own insights screen as proof. We store the image.

Technical records. A session token while you are signed in, and an audit log of significant actions on your account.

If you connect your Instagram account

Connecting Instagram is optional. The portal works without it, and nothing about your payouts depends on it. If you do connect, you are sent to Instagram to log in — we never see or store your Instagram password.

Instagram then gives us a token that lets us read, on a read-only basis, two things:

  • Your professional account's basic profile (instagram_business_basic) — the account's ID and username, so we can confirm the connected account is yours and match it to the videos you have submitted.
  • View counts on your videos (instagram_business_manage_insights) — so we can read the view count on videos you have submitted to an Edgeform campaign, including trial reels whose counts are not public.

We use this for one purpose: reading view counts on videos you submitted, so you do not have to screenshot them every week. We do not post anything, do not read or send your direct messages, do not touch your comments, and do not look at videos you have not submitted to a campaign. We do not use it to build an advertising profile of you.

You can disconnect at any time from Settings in the portal, or from Instagram's own Apps and websites screen. Disconnecting deletes the token immediately and we stop reading anything. View counts already recorded stay on your earnings history, because they are the basis of payments already made to you.

Why we hold any of it

To run the programme you signed up for: to check submitted videos are eligible, to count views, to calculate what you are owed, to pay you, and to keep records our accountants and tax obligations require.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Who else touches it

  • Cloudflare hosts the portal, the API and the database.
  • Meta Platforms, if you connect Instagram — data flows from Instagram to us under the permissions above.
  • Our clients, who see which videos ran on their campaign and how those videos performed. They do not receive your contact details or payout information.
  • Payment providers you ask us to pay you through.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account and earnings records are kept while you are in the programme and for seven years afterwards, which is the retention our financial-record obligations set. Instagram tokens are deleted the moment you disconnect. Screenshots are kept alongside the week they evidence.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your data at any time — see Data Deletion for how, and what we are obliged to keep.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data we hold on you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will action it within 30 days.
