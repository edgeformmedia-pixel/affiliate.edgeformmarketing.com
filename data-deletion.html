Deleting your data

Last updated 20 September 2026

There are two different things you might want to remove. They are not the same, so pick the one you mean.

1. Disconnect Instagram only

This stops Edgeform reading anything from your Instagram account, and keeps your affiliate account and earnings intact.

  • Sign in to the portal, open Settings, and choose Disconnect in the Instagram section; or
  • In the Instagram app, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites and remove Edgeform View Tracker.

Either route deletes the access token immediately and we stop reading your account. If you disconnect from Instagram's side, Instagram notifies us automatically and we delete the token without you doing anything else.

After disconnecting, view counts on trial reels are no longer read automatically, so you will need to upload a screenshot of your insights each week again.

2. Delete your whole affiliate account

Email team@edgeformmarketing.com from the address on your account with the subject Data deletion request.

We will confirm within 3 working days and complete the deletion within 30 days. You do not need to give a reason.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email, phone number, country and social handles
  • Your payout method details
  • Your Instagram connection and its access token
  • Insight screenshots you uploaded
  • Your sign-in sessions

What we have to keep, and why

We cannot delete records of payments already made to you. Financial and tax law requires us to retain them for seven years. After deletion these records no longer identify you personally — they are reduced to the amount, the date and a reference number.

Videos you submitted also stay associated with the campaign they ran on, because our client's campaign reporting depends on them. Your personal details are unlinked from them.
